Twin Tumble – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Twin Tumble (package name com.CraftUz.TwinTumble, the “App”) by CraftUz (“we”, “us”) handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

Game progress (levels, stars, coins, items, theme and sound settings, language) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob: interstitial ads between levels and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for extra coins. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and use:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • ad interaction and diagnostic data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped, crash and performance data).

This data is processed by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy. More information about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices and consent

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice later using the “Privacy settings” button on the start screen of the App.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).
  • You can play without watching rewarded ads; they are always optional.

4. Children

The App is intended for general audiences aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will help to resolve it.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google (AdMob), as described above.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because game data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings. Data processed by Google for advertising is kept according to Google’s policies.

7. Security

The App communicates with ad servers over secure connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to limit the data the App handles.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
